## 🎯 The Problem

Businesses spend money generating leads, but many opportunities are lost because:

- Leads are not contacted quickly
- Missed calls are not followed up
- Website enquiries are forgotten
- Social media enquiries remain unanswered
- Follow-ups are inconsistent
- Sales teams cannot easily identify high-value prospects

LEADRESCUE AI is designed around solving this lead-leakage problem.

---

## 💡 The Solution

LEADRESCUE AI provides a centralized revenue recovery interface for:

**Capture → Respond → Qualify → Follow Up → Convert**

The platform demonstrates how AI-assisted workflows can help businesses recover opportunities that might otherwise be missed.
